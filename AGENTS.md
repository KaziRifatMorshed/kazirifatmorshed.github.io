# Repository Rules and Instructions

## Privacy & Security Guidelines
- **Do NOT publish or add personal phone / contact numbers** to the website or any public HTML/markdown files under any circumstances.
- **Do NOT publish full, specific residential or permanent addresses** (e.g. holding numbers, road numbers, area sub-streets). Keep location information strictly high-level/general (e.g. `Khulna-9100, Bangladesh`).
- Email, GitHub, LinkedIn, Telegram handle, WhatsApp link, and social profiles are acceptable, but raw phone numbers and detailed home addresses must remain private for security and privacy protection.
- When syncing website content with updated CVs, resumes, or documents, always filter out phone numbers, full street addresses, and any sensitive personal identifiers.
